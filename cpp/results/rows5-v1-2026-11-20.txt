Engine measurements on the 5-row board (board-rows package V1), 2026-11-20
==========================================================================

Machine: Apple M3 Max (16 cores), macOS, Node.js v24.22.1, JDK 25.0.3 (Zulu). Engines: web/public/engines built from the branch
board-rows (scripts/web/build-wasm.zsh, scripts/web/build-teavm.zsh); C++ = engine-cpp.wasm, Java = TeaVM Wasm GC (engine-java.wasm).
The 5-row board: RuleSet.TriangleRows FIVE, 15-cell houses, 15 pieces per player, move limit 250 plies (2 players) / 375 (3 players).

Tools
-----
- scripts/web/measure-rows5.zsh (and measure-rows5.mjs): seeded self-play of the browser players in Node. C++ plays every move; at each
  compared ply TeaVM chooses for the same history, and move, score, depth and nodes must agree. 4 random opening plies per game
  (seed 5000 + game). After a win: the distance of the result as web/src/result/distance.ts computes it (solo race of the loser,
  beam 60; three players: playout at 500 nodes per candidate to second place), cap 400 or the move limit.
- search/src/test/java/sternhalma/search/Rows5MoveLimitProbe.java: JVM self-play of the same browser players with a move limit of
  1500 plies, to see whether 250/375 cuts games that would still end. Data: rows5-v1-limit-probe-2026-11-20.tsv (after the fix below),
  rows5-v1-limit-probe-before-fix-2026-11-20.tsv (before).

Commands (W = a scratch directory):
  scripts/web/measure-rows5.zsh --work W --rows 5 --players P --levels N --games 8 --java-plies 400     (P 2,3; N 1000, 5000, 20000)
  scripts/web/measure-rows5.zsh --work W --rows 5 --players P --levels 100000 --games 6 --java-plies 120
  scripts/web/measure-rows5.zsh --work W --rows 5 --players P --levels 500000 --camps closed --games 2 --java-plies 30

Results in short
----------------
1. Finding and fix: the 3-player browser player of the 5-row board made no progress. With the first build of the branch, 139 of 141 (99 %)
   3-player games on 5 rows ended at the move limit, and in the JVM probe 35 of 36 games still had no piece in any goal after 1500
   plies. The 2-player player reached the limit in 39 of 151 games (26 %). Cause: the linear feature dsumPhase (dsum difference times
   the total remaining distance of both sides / 100) has a positive weight and dsum a negative one; with 15 pieces the total is about
   1.35 times larger than with 10, and a step towards the goal then lowered the score (static check: a 4-step move of player 0 after
   9 plies scored +1 on 5 rows, +61 on 4 rows). The fix counts that total in units of 10 pieces (times 10/15 on 5 rows;
   LinearEvaluator.phaseDistance in Java, LinearEvaluator::combine in C++). 4-row scores are bit-identical (all 4-row fingerprints
   and goldens unchanged). After the fix every game below ends with a win.
2. Move limits: keep 250 and 375. After the fix, 0 of 345 5-row games in Node and 0 of 288 games in the JVM probe (limit 1500)
   ended by the limit. The longest 5-row games: 2 players 223 plies (89 % of 250; the JVM probe at most 174), 3 players 302 plies
   (81 % of 375; the probe at most 256).
3. Score bound: the largest |score| below the race band on 5 rows is 925 (3 players, long hop, 20000 nodes); 2 players at most 903.
   RACE_BASE is 5000 and the aspiration cap 2000. No 5-row move scored in the race band.
4. Speed: a move on 5 rows costs 1.2 to 1.9 times the 4-row move at the same budget (table "Speed pass";
   1.5 for 2 players with adjacent jumps at 100,000 and 500,000 nodes). At the top level (500,000
   nodes) a 2-player move takes about 0.54 s (adjacent) and 1.0 s (long hop) in C++ wasm, 1.0 s and 1.7 s in TeaVM Wasm GC.
5. Parity: TeaVM and C++ agree on move, score, depth and node count at all 45,374 compared plies of the main run and all 1,600 of
   the speed pass (before the fix: all 75,964).
6. Result-card caps: keep SOLO_CAP = 100 and PLAYOUT_CAP = 60. Solo races after 2-player wins on 5 rows took at most 9 moves
   (adjacent) and, except for 4 capped races, at most 17 (long hop); 3-player playouts to second place took at most 17 plies. The 4
   capped long-hop races are positions in which a piece of the loser stays in its own start house, which is the winner's goal: the
   winner's 14 pieces enclose it and never move again, so the race cannot end (the "goal full and at least one own piece" rule).
   Such a race reaches the cap whatever the cap is; at 100 moves it costs about 0.3 s in Node.
7. 4-row regression: the benchmark fingerprint (40 plies, 20,000 nodes) is unchanged for cpp-wasm, java-js and java-wasmgc
   (scripts/web/bench.zsh, CPU/wall 1.00 to 1.01, load average about 3). An interleaved A/B of the 4-row benchmark game against
   the build of commit 798cab9 (before the board-rows work), 16 games each, load average about 3: cpp-wasm 1,800,565 against
   1,836,383 nodes/s (0.980) and in the reverse order 0.983 (1.017 inverted); TeaVM Wasm GC 1,015,922 against 995,819 (1.020).
   So the C++ wasm engine is about 2 % slower on 4 rows since the board-rows work; TeaVM is not slower.
